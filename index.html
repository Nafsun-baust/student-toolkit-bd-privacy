Privacy Policy for Student Toolkit BD

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Student Toolkit BD is an Android study planner provided by Md Nafsun Kabir. This policy explains what information the app stores on your device and what information may be processed by its advertising provider.

Information stored on your device

The app works without an account. Your chosen name, display preferences, study tasks, class and exam entries, and saved semester GPA courses are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this study content.

If you choose to export a backup, the app writes a file containing your name, preferences, tasks, classes and exams to a location you choose. You control where that file is stored or shared. If you restore a backup, the app reads the file you select and replaces the corresponding local app data.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app schedules reminders for study tasks and exams on your device. You can change notification permission and delivery settings in Android settings.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner advertisements. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic data, and device or advertising identifiers. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This advertising data is handled by Google and its partners; your locally saved study content is not sent to our server for advertising.

Where required, the app asks for your advertising consent before requesting ads and provides a Privacy choices option in Settings when available. You can also manage your advertising ID through Android settings. See Google's Privacy Policy for details of Google's practices.

Data deletion and retention

Settings > Clear planner data deletes saved tasks, classes and exams from the app on this device. It does not delete your chosen name, display preferences, saved GPA courses or any backup file you exported. To remove all local app data, use Android's Clear storage option for Student Toolkit BD or uninstall the app. Delete any exported backup separately from wherever you saved it. We do not retain your study content on our servers.

Security

Study data remains in the app's local storage unless you choose to export a backup. Backups you save are plain-text JSON files, so store them in a place you trust. Google states that data transmitted by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. The current version and its effective date will be shown on this page. For privacy questions, contact mdnafsunkabir@gmail.com.
